Why we started

Harder than anyone told us.

My wife and I both work, and we went into parenthood with a picture of how it would go. It turned out to be harder than either of us expected, in more ways than I can count.

We've had great nights. We've also had the worst nights, the ones that spiral on no sleep, then a day at work on cup after cup of coffee.

As a dad you feel everything. You're protecting your partner and your baby while your own life changes overnight. And half the time you're not sure who to ask, or how to actually help.

Fatherhood FC is for that. A club where dads come together to build a community and a support network, from preparation and pregnancy to birth and beyond.

Alex, founder and new dad, Yorkshire
